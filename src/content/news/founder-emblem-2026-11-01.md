Hunmeng has a new emblem that belongs to a single account: the **Founder of Hunmeng**.

### The emblem

A pavilion rises above an open book, with a quill and a stylus crossed beneath it — a world built from the pages of a story. A crown of golden rays, laurel branches and emerald crystals frame the composition.

</div>

### Where it appears

- In the founder's reader profile, as a large emblem with a **Founder** ribbon.
- As a small badge on the founder's avatar in the profile and in the site header.
- On the **Founder of Hunmeng** title ribbon when that title is equipped.

### What stays the same

The emblem is not earned through reading and cannot be unlocked by other accounts. Reader XP, ranks, Reader's Path, titles, achievements and Halloween Reading Week rules are unchanged.
