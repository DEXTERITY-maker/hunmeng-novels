The News section has been redesigned to match the rest of Hunmeng.

### An illustrated feed

The page opens with the autumn landscape used in reader profiles. Each post is shown as a card with its own illustration, and the newest post appears as a wide featured card. Category filters are now rounded tabs.

### New artwork inside the articles

Posts about new emblems, ranks and event awards now show the artwork itself. For example, the Halloween Reading Week post presents all three award emblems:

Earlier posts about the nine reader ranks, the Reader's Path emblems, the Hunmeng emblem and the reader profile now include their illustrations as well.

### Easier reading

Articles have a large cover, clearer headings and more comfortable line spacing. At the end of each article, three more recent posts are suggested.

### What stays the same

Post categories, reactions and links to individual posts are unchanged.
