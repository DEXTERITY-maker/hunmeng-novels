The library and signed-in reader profile now share the visual style used across Hunmeng.

### Library improvements

- **Illustrated library banner.** A panoramic landscape adapts to light, dark and sepia themes, with a compact mobile version.
- **Clearer reading shelves.** Reading, Read later, Read and Dropped now appear as compact cards with icons and live counts.
- **Redesigned book rows.** Covers, authors, saved chapter positions, progress bars and dates are easier to scan. Stories with dedicated artwork display it beside the book information on larger screens.
- **Improved empty states.** Empty shelves and on-device storage guidance have a cleaner presentation.

### Reader profile improvements

- **Updated profile header.** The selected avatar and verified rank emblem appear together above the profile sections.
- **Consistent cards.** Profile settings, recent reading, rank progress, Reader's Path, achievements, titles and XP history use more readable panels.
- **Recent reading covers.** Saved novels now show their covers alongside the existing local chapter progress.

### What remains unchanged

Shelf counts, book moves and removal, reading-position restoration, local storage, optional account synchronization, sign-in and sign-out, profile editing, avatar choices, selected titles and pinned badges work as before. Verified reader ranks and XP remain separate from Reader's Path and MP. Achievement rules, rewards, levels and all underlying data have not changed.
