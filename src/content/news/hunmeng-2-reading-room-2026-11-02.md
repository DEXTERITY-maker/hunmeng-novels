**Hunmeng 2.0** introduces a calmer home for discovering stories and continuing your reading.

### What's new

- **A refreshed reading room.** The homepage highlights published books, the newest chapters, editorial news and curated external references, clearly identified as such.
- **Better discovery.** The catalog features a redesigned search experience, quick genre filters and cleaner cards. Searching can also match book descriptions and themes.
- **A more comfortable reader.** Chapter headings and controls have a quieter presentation, mobile spacing has improved, and familiar reading preferences continue to work.
- **Editorial workflow preview.** Authorized editors can explore the separate `/v2/admin/` workspace. Existing staff permissions, rights verification and publication checks are unchanged.

### What stays the same

Your account, library, progress, bookmarks, reading preferences and published chapter links remain where they were. No new third-party chapters are published as part of this design update. External novels remain references to their original reading sources, not content hosted by Hunmeng.

The novel details, user profile and writing studio continue using their existing interfaces. A separate design preview remains accessible at `/v2/`.
