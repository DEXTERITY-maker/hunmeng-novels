**New chapter notifications** are now available in the official Hunmeng Telegram channel.

When a new chapter becomes available, the channel shares a short English announcement with the novel title, chapter number and title, and a direct link to read it on Hunmeng. Notices cover both daily scheduled releases and newly published chapters from the main collection. No chapter text or story spoilers are included.

To follow new releases, select **Get new chapters in Telegram** on a novel page or at the end of a chapter, or visit [Hunmeng on Telegram](https://t.me/hunmengnovels).

The notification service runs separately from publication and does not change chapter availability, reading progress, shelves or reader preferences. Previously published chapters are not reposted when the service starts.
