The editorial workflow for bringing authorized web novels into the **Hunmeng reading platform** has been expanded.

### What is new

- **Batch chapter preparation.** Authorized editorial staff can validate and import up to ten supplied chapters at a time into private drafts.
- **Rights checks.** Import requires current, documented permission to translate and republish the work. Source links alone do not unlock publishing.
- **Optional machine translation.** A separate, manually triggered tool can prepare translation drafts when the translation service is configured; model usage is not automatic.
- **Human review remains required.** Drafts must be checked and approved before the existing publisher can create reading pages and chapter announcements.

### What stays unchanged

The Hunmeng reader, reading progress and catalog remain available. No unlicensed chapter texts were imported by this update. **Highway Survival** remains an external reference pending permission for on-site translation and publication.
