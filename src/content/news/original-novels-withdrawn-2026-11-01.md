Two original Hunmeng novels have been withdrawn from the public library: **Comprehension Against the Heavens** and **The Latency of Tomorrow**. Their public novel pages, published chapters, cover images and release schedules are no longer available.

### Catalog availability

The catalog currently has no published novels. Search, genres, filters and the reading interface remain ready for future additions. Previously published news about the two stories has been updated to avoid links to removed chapters.

### What stays the same

This content withdrawal does not reset accounts, reader XP, ranks, achievements, titles or site settings. Reader progress and saved shelves are not erased; records for unavailable novels may remain on a reader's device, but withdrawn chapter pages can no longer be opened.
