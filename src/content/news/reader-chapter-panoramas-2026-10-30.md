I refreshed the beginning of each chapter. Chapters of *Comprehension Against the Heavens* now open with their own mountain landscape, which changes between day and night with your theme. *The Latency of Tomorrow* has its rainy Shenzhen scene in every theme. On a phone, the illustration is shorter and the chapter title stays clear. If a story has no illustration, the heading stays simple.

I also made the reading controls softer, with a lightly blurred panel and a fade above it so the chapter text stays easy to read. On phones, the back button, logo space, settings, bookmark, contents and more options fit on one line. The reading progress bar remains directly beneath that header.

I **did not change** the chapter text, reading fonts, text width, position saving and restoring, Read later, focus mode, settings, chapter navigation, scheduled releases or reading rewards. Your saved reading progress and preferences stay the same.
