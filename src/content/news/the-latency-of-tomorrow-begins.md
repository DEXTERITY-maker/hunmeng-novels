Shenzhen, 2026. A missing brother. Fourteen minutes omitted from an industrial report. A future whose consequences reach far beyond one family.

The first twenty chapters of [The Latency of Tomorrow](/novel/the-latency-of-tomorrow/) are available to read in English.

The novel is being written as one 247-chapter volume. When the complete manuscript is ready, the release will open with 50 chapters; one further chapter will become available each day at 12:00 Moscow time. That daily schedule has not started yet.
