Reader profiles have received a visual refinement inspired by Hunmeng's landscape artwork. The profile header now features a wider illustrated scene, a prominent avatar, a decorative equipped-title ribbon, and a clearer rank medallion with its XP progress.

The four verified statistics, profile navigation and Overview cards now use warm parchment surfaces, subtle bronze borders, forest-green accents and more readable typography. Reading shortcuts, the next milestone, event awards and recent XP remain organized in their existing tabs. Desktop and mobile layouts adapt to light, dark and sepia themes.

Reader XP, verified ranks, equipped titles, Reader's Path, MP, achievement eligibility, reading history, shelves, saved profile details and account synchronization are unchanged.
