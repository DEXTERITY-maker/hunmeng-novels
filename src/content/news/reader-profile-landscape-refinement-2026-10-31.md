Reader profiles now open onto an autumn landscape: maple branches, waterfalls and mountain pavilions stretch across the full width of the page, with a night version for the dark theme.

### A header that feels like a place

The avatar is larger and framed by a golden laurel wreath. The equipped title appears on a forest-green ribbon, and the current rank medallion sits beside the level and XP progress, directly on the landscape.

### Warmer cards

The four verified statistics, profile navigation and Overview cards use parchment surfaces, bronze borders, forest-green accents and serif headings. Faint ink-wash mountains decorate the corners of each card. Continue reading, Next goal, Event awards and Recent XP keep their places in the Overview tab.

### On every screen

Desktop and phone layouts adapt to the light, dark and sepia themes. On phones the landscape sits at the top, with the avatar, nickname and rank below it.

### What stays the same

Reader XP, verified ranks, equipped titles, Reader's Path, MP, achievement eligibility, reading history, shelves, saved profile details and account synchronization are unchanged.
