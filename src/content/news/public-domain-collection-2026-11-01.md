The first collection of classic fantasy, horror, science-fiction and adventure novels can now be read directly on Hunmeng.
The original English texts are public-domain works, with editorial editions whose contributors dedicate their work under CC0.
No copyrighted contemporary web-novel chapters or translations were copied.

### Open the books

- [Frankenstein; or, The Modern Prometheus](/novel/frankenstein/) — Mary Shelley
- [Dracula](/novel/dracula/) — Bram Stoker
- [The Time Machine](/novel/the-time-machine/) — H. G. Wells
- [The War of the Worlds](/novel/the-war-of-the-worlds/) — H. G. Wells
- [The Wonderful Wizard of Oz](/novel/the-wonderful-wizard-of-oz/) — L. Frank Baum
- [Treasure Island](/novel/treasure-island/) — Robert Louis Stevenson
- [The Strange Case of Dr. Jekyll and Mr. Hyde](/novel/the-strange-case-of-dr-jekyll-and-mr-hyde/) — Robert Louis Stevenson
- [The Picture of Dorian Gray](/novel/the-picture-of-dorian-gray/) — Oscar Wilde
