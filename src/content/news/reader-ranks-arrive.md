Your reading journey now has milestones of its own.

**Reader levels** run from 1 to 500, and nine automatically earned ranks mark your progress: Iron, Bronze, Silver, Gold, **Dark Gold**, Platinum, Diamond, Legendary and Mythical.

Each rank has its own emblem. Signed-in readers can view their experience, rank gallery, separate titles and achievements in **[My profile](/account/)**.

**How experience works:** published chapters can earn XP after an eligible reading session is completed. The server prevents a chapter from granting the same award repeatedly. Merely opening a page does not grant XP.

Every reader begins with the **Novice Reader** title. More titles have their own goals, separate from ranks. Goals requiring moderated reviews or a verified cross-device library remain locked until those systems are available.

Your existing reading position and library remain on this device; account-linked XP and titles do not change that. You can continue reading as a guest without registering.
