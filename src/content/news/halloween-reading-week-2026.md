**Halloween Reading Week** runs from **1 to 7 November 2026**, Moscow time.

Signed-in readers can join the event with one button on the [Halloween Reading Week page](/events/halloween-2026/). Participation is voluntary. The leaderboard shows the nickname from the reader's profile, which can be changed in **Edit profile**; email addresses and account identities are not shown. Readers can leave before the event ends.

The leaderboard counts chapters verified by the existing reading-session reward system during the event week, including eligible chapters completed before joining. Rankings are based on completed chapters, with ties decided by who reached the count earlier.

- **First place:** 3,000 XP, the **Lantern of the Long Night** title, and a gold Halloween emblem.
- **Second place:** 2,000 XP, the **Keeper of the Autumn Moon** title, and a silver Halloween emblem.
- **Third place:** 1,000 XP, the **Reader of the Falling Leaves** title, and a bronze Halloween emblem.

A minimum of one verified chapter is required to receive a prize. Hunmeng team members may take part, but prizes go only to readers. The results and awards are finalized after the event by an administrator. Awarded titles can be equipped in the reader profile.

Reading XP rules, reader ranks, Reader's Path, MP, chapter progress and library shelves remain unchanged. Event prizes are additional verified XP awards.
