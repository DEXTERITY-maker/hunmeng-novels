We've released a small interface update to make everyday browsing on **Hunmeng** feel smoother and more responsive.

### What's improved

- **Smoother catalog results.** When you change filters or move between catalog pages, the results appear with a brief, gentle transition rather than an abrupt change.
- **Clearer loading feedback.** If the catalog's search data takes longer than expected, a small loading indicator appears. Existing novel cards stay visible while the data loads.
- **Subtle interaction details.** Buttons, catalog filters and news-category controls now offer more consistent visual feedback when you use them.
- **Motion preferences respected.** The new transitions are reduced or disabled when your device requests less motion.

### What stays the same

This is a **visual and usability update**. No novels, chapters, reader XP, achievements, ranks or titles have been changed. Your library, reading progress and account information remain untouched.

The changes are available on desktop and mobile, across light, dark and sepia themes.
