The **reader profile** now opens with a compact overview designed for desktop and mobile reading. The updated header brings the reader's avatar, nickname, bio, equipped title, verified rank and XP progress together above the profile navigation.

Four verified totals — **Chapters**, **Novels finished**, **Reading days** and **Achievements** — replace the long mix of account and on-device library counters. Profile information is organized into **Overview**, **Progress**, **Titles & achievements** and **XP history** tabs.

**Overview** includes a direct link to continue reading, the nearest verified achievement goal, available event awards and recent XP activity. The Progress tab retains the current and next rank, the six Reader's Path medallions and a link to the full nine-rank gallery. Profile details can now be edited from a dedicated dialog near the top of the page.

The redesigned profile uses Hunmeng's existing daytime and nighttime landscape artwork, ranks and Reader's Path images. Reading settings and account management remain available from the existing account navigation.

**What remains unchanged:** verified experience and rank calculations, titles and achievement eligibility, Reader's Path, MP, reading progress, account metadata storage, optional cloud synchronization, shelves and chapter rewards.
