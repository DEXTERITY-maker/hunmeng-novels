The **Reader's Path** has received a full visual upgrade. All six mastery emblems now share one artistic direction, with **Hunmeng Legend** as the reference for materials, lighting, detail and craftsmanship.


### One collection, six distinct identities

Every mastery step has its own jewel-toned, book-inspired illustration:

- **Novice Reader** (levels 1–4) — an emerald sprout and an open book mark the start of the journey.
- **Reader** (5–9) — cool silver, elegant foliage and a more developed book motif.
- **Experienced Reader** (10–19) — sapphire-blue crystals, luminous pages and layered metalwork.
- **Connoisseur** (20–34) — emerald-green gemstones and refined ornamental details.
- **Master** (35–49) — rich gold, an eight-pointed star and a more prestigious silhouette.
- **Hunmeng Legend** (50–500) — a crown, purple crystals and the most intricate platinum-and-gold composition.

These are **individual, transparent 640 × 640 WebP illustrations**, designed to look consistent on desktop, tablet and phone. The illustrations appear in your profile’s Reader's Path gallery and in the celebration shown when a new mastery step is unlocked.

### What stays the same

The update is visual. **Your XP, existing progress, the six level thresholds and the separate nine-rank system do not change.** All earned achievements and equipped titles remain intact.
