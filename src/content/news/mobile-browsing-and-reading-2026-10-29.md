A focused update makes **Hunmeng easier to use on phones**, from finding a novel to settling into a chapter.

### What's improved

- **A more welcoming home page.** The illustrated introduction now takes up less space on smaller screens. Search, reading shortcuts, and the featured novel are easier to reach, with the *New novels* section appearing sooner.
- **Faster access to the catalog.** A more compact banner and filter panel bring the first novel into view sooner. Search by title or author and a **Sort by** selector are available immediately; additional filters remain expandable.
- **Clearer chapter information.** Novel pages now show **published chapters** and **chapters planned in total** separately, with a link to the release schedule when available.
- **More comfortable reading controls.** The mobile reading toolbar has refined spacing, larger touch targets, and more room below the chapter text, including support for phone safe-area insets.
- **Motion that respects your preferences.** Mobile content remains readable without waiting for introductory animations, and reduced-motion preferences are respected.

### What stays the same

This is a **layout and usability update**, not a change to the reading or reward systems. No novels or chapters were added as part of this release. Your library, reading progress, XP, ranks, and account data have not been migrated or reset. Requests for new novels are **not** enabled by this update.

These improvements are designed to make browsing and reading more comfortable on every visit.
