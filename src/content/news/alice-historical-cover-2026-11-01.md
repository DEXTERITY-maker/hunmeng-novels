The Hunmeng edition of **Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland**, by **Lewis Carroll**, now displays the historic red-and-gold cover of the 1865 first edition instead of a placeholder.

The cover image is from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Alice%27s_Adventures_in_Wonderland_cover_%281865%29.jpg), where it is marked as public domain. The reading text remains sourced from the [Standard Ebooks edition](https://standardebooks.org/ebooks/lewis-carroll/alices-adventures-in-wonderland/john-tenniel).
