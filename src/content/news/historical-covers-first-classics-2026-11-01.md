Nine public-domain classics in the Hunmeng library now display authentic historical images from original or early editions, drawn from Wikimedia Commons.

The update replaces generic placeholder artwork. **Frankenstein** and **Treasure Island** use historical **title pages**, rather than claiming those scans are original front-board covers.

### Updated books

- [Frankenstein; or, The Modern Prometheus](/novel/frankenstein/) — 1818 title page of the 1818 first edition ([source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Frankenstein%201818%20edition%20title%20page.jpg))
- [Dracula](/novel/dracula/) — 1897 original yellow-cloth cover of the 1897 first edition ([source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dracula-First-Edition-1897.jpg))
- [The Time Machine](/novel/the-time-machine/) — 1895 front cover of the William Heinemann 1895 edition ([source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The%20Time%20Machine%20(Heinemann%20text)%20-%20front%20cover.jpg))
- [The War of the Worlds](/novel/the-war-of-the-worlds/) — 1898 original cover of the 1898 first book edition ([source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The%20War%20of%20the%20Worlds%20first%20edition.jpg))
- [The Wonderful Wizard of Oz](/novel/the-wonderful-wizard-of-oz/) — 1900 W. W. Denslow's original first-edition binding, 1900 ([source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The%20Wonderful%20Wizard%20of%20Oz%20first%20edition%20cover.jpg))
- [Treasure Island](/novel/treasure-island/) — 1883 title page of the first 1883 Cassell book edition ([source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Treasure%20Island%20(title%20page%2C%201883).webp))
- [The Strange Case of Dr. Jekyll and Mr. Hyde](/novel/the-strange-case-of-dr-jekyll-and-mr-hyde/) — 1886 historical 1886 publisher's cover/title page ([source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Strange%20Case%20of%20Dr%20Jekyll%20and%20Mr%20Hyde%20001.jpg))
- [The Picture of Dorian Gray](/novel/the-picture-of-dorian-gray/) — 1891 first 1891 book-edition cover, Houghton Library, Harvard University ([source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Houghton%20HEW%2012.10.15%20-%20The%20Picture%20of%20Dorian%20Gray%2C%201891%20(cover).jpg))
- [The Island of Doctor Moreau](/novel/the-island-of-doctor-moreau/) — 1896 front cover of the William Heinemann 1896 first edition ([source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:IslandOfDrMoreau.JPG))

Only the cover assets and their book metadata were changed. All chapter texts, reading progress, and other reader features remain unchanged.
