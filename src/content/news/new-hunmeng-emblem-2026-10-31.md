Hunmeng has a new emblem. An open book opens onto a path that winds up to a pavilion beneath a golden sun — a story leading into another world.

### Where it appears

- The site header next to the Hunmeng name.
- The chapter reader header on phones, beside the back button.
- The browser tab icon and the home-screen icon when the site is added to a phone.

### What stays the same

Accounts, library shelves, reading progress, ratings, XP and ranks remain unchanged. No new chapters were published with this update.
