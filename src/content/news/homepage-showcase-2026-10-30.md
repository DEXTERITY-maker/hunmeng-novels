I have updated the homepage to make it easier to discover what to read next.

The welcome banner now uses our castle landscape. It changes to the night version when you switch to the dark theme, and the text stays readable over a gentle background. I gave the featured stories a new carousel with real novels from the site. You can move between them with the arrows, dots, keyboard or a swipe on your phone. There is no automatic switching. I also added a golden **Read now** link for the selected novel.

I made three separate cards for browsing genres, checking the latest chapters and finding new releases. Each has its own little illustration. The **New releases** section now has cleaner book cards with their genres, descriptions and actual publication details.

I did not change the stories, covers, chapters or their data. Search still goes to the catalog. **Continue reading**, latest chapters, news, popular stories, recommendations, saved reading progress, library shelves and ratings still work the same way. I did not publish any new chapters with this design update.
