**Paragraph bookmarks** are now available in the chapter reader. A bookmark saves a specific passage rather than adding an entire novel to a reading shelf.

To save a place, scroll to the paragraph and choose **Bookmark this paragraph** from the **⋯** menu. On desktop, the small flag beside a paragraph provides a shortcut. A saved paragraph displays a marker. Choose **Remove bookmark** or the marker to remove it.

Open **Contents**, then select **Bookmarks** to see saved passages from the current novel, including the chapter, a short quotation and the date. Select **Open** to return to a passage, or **Remove** to delete its bookmark. If the chapter text has changed and the passage cannot be matched safely, the reader opens the chapter at its beginning and displays a notice.

Bookmarks are saved on the current device. Readers who enable account cloud sync can carry bookmarks to their other synced devices. The existing **Read later** shelf and its header icon are separate from paragraph bookmarks. Reading progress, shelves, reading preferences, XP, ranks and rewards remain unchanged.
