The reader profile now has a calmer layout inspired by Hunmeng's light landscape design. The selected avatar, nickname, biography, equipped title and verified rank appear together at the top, with **Edit profile** and **My library** close at hand.

Four verified statistics summarize completed chapters, finished novels, reading days and unlocked achievements. The profile is organized into **Overview**, **Progress**, **Titles & achievements**, and **XP history** tabs, so the entire reading journey no longer appears as one continuous page.

Overview brings together a recent reading shortcut, the next achievement, any earned event awards or an ongoing event invitation, and a preview of the latest three XP entries. Progress gives a more compact look at the current rank, the next milestone and the six Reader's Path emblems. Full rank illustrations remain available on the ranks page. The title and achievement filters are preserved, and XP history now displays known novel titles instead of internal slugs.

Profile details are edited in a dialog, using the same account information and save operation as before. Phone layouts stack content and keep account and profile navigation close to the top.

Verified XP awards, level and rank calculations, title eligibility, Reader's Path, MP, reading progress, library shelves, reader preferences and account synchronization remain unchanged.
