Hunmeng's latest visual refresh brings together the atmosphere of a private library and the clarity of a modern reading website.

### What's changed

- **A more distinctive look.** Refined headings, emerald accents, subtle gold details and softer paper-like surfaces now work together across the site.
- **A more focused home page.** The illustrated welcome area, search and featured novel have clearer emphasis, while the latest chapters, recommendations and news follow a more consistent editorial layout.
- **A cleaner catalog.** Novel covers, search controls, sorting, filters and result cards have a unified design that keeps browsing simple on desktop and phone.
- **Polished story pages.** Novel details and chapter information are easier to scan, without changing which chapters are available.
- **Consistent news, library and account screens.** Cards, navigation, hover and keyboard-focus states are easier to recognize.
- **Reading stays comfortable.** The chapter reader keeps its familiar layout and controls, with a small visual refinement to its toolbar.

### Your preferences still matter

The design adapts to light, dark and sepia appearances and respects reduced-motion preferences. The refresh is visual only: **your account, library, reading progress, reviews, XP and ranks remain unchanged.**

No new novels or chapters were published as part of this design update. The novel-request system is not part of this release.
