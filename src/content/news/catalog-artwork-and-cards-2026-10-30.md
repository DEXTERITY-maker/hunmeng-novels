I have refreshed the Hunmeng catalog to make finding a story more enjoyable.

### What I changed

- I added a fantasy landscape at the top of the catalog. It changes with the light and dark themes.
- I gave each novel card its own background illustration when one is available. The image stays on the right so the title and description remain easy to read.
- I made the genre, tags, published chapter count, status, and update date easier to find.
- I made the **Read novel** button clearer and adjusted the cards for small phone screens.
- I kept the same catalog banner and novel card design on the paginated catalog pages.

### What stays the same

You can still search by title or author, sort the novels, include or exclude tags, reset filters, and share a link with your chosen filters. Pagination and empty search results still work. I have not added any novels, chapters, ratings, or views with this update.

Your library, reading progress, reviews, account, XP, and ranks are not changed by this visual refresh.
