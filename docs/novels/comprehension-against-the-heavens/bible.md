# Comprehension Against the Heavens — story bible

Original web novel by **Hunmeng**, written in English for hunmeng.net. 972 chapters in 12 volumes of 81 chapters.
Chapters 1–149 went live on 2026-10-25; from 2026-10-26 five chapters are released every day (see `scheduled/README.md`).

Read this file and `outline.md` before writing any chapter. Keep every fact here consistent. When a chapter
introduces a new lasting fact (a name, a technique, an address, a rule of the power system), add it here.

## Style guide

- Third person limited, past tense, almost always Ye Xuan's point of view. Short cutaways to villains are allowed at
  chapter ends as hooks (as in chapter 3), at most one per chapter.
- Web-novel rhythm: short paragraphs (1–4 sentences), clear action, inner thoughts in *italics*, light humor, a hook or
  turn at the end of every chapter. Scene breaks are a line with `* * *`.
- Length: 1,500–2,200 words per chapter. Every chapter must move the plot or a relationship; no recap-only chapters.
- Ye Xuan's voice: dry, self-aware about web-novel clichés ("of course the truck"), methodical (he makes numbered
  "rules"), stubborn, kind without being naive. He thinks in meters and kilograms; Americans around him use feet and
  pounds. He swears mildly in Mandarin ("heaven and earth", "aiya" is Mrs. Tang's).
- Show comprehension visually: the world "shifts" and he sees threads/lines/patterns of light. Cost = pressure behind
  the eyes, nosebleeds, exhaustion; it gets cheaper as he cultivates. Never let him comprehend everything for free.
- No real brands as plot devices, no real public figures, no copyrighted characters (no Marvel/DC). Real places in
  New York are fine (Mott Street, Columbus Park, Pier 17, Red Hook, Manhattan Bridge, the Bronx, Flushing).
- Content level: action violence without gore; no sexual content (the romance is slow-burn and PG-13).
- Description field: one or two sentences, 10–200 characters, no double quotes inside.

## Premise

Ye Xuan (叶玄), 18, from Hangzhou, dies saving a child from a truck in the rain eleven days after the gaokao. A bell
rings "beneath the world" and a voice says "This is where it begins." He wakes in New York, in another Earth, in the
body of a namesake: a 19-year-old international engineering student at Hudson Polytechnic Institute who lived in a
small room above the Golden Lotus bakery on Mott Street, Chinatown. The previous Ye Xuan died after a night at Pier 17
three days earlier. Our Ye Xuan has none of his memories, only scattered images (a lecture hall, a woman laughing,
black water, ozone).

Twelve years before the story, the sky over the whole planet burned white for seven minutes: the **Glow** (officially
the Global Luminous Event). Since then about one in a hundred thousand people **Awaken** with a gift.

## The truth of the world (revealed gradually; do not reveal ahead of the outline)

- The Glow poured **qi** (spiritual energy) back into a world from which it had been sealed. Earth once had
  cultivators; about nine thousand years ago the **Celestial Court**, a power beyond the sky, sealed Earth behind the
  **Heaven's Seal** to farm it and keep humanity weak. The Glow was the first crack in the Seal.
- Awakened are people whose bodies soaked up qi by accident. Each gift is one crude, self-burned **Dao pattern**.
  Gifts cannot be taught or transferred (common belief). Pushing a gift too hard causes **burnout**: patterns fray,
  qi overheats, the body crystallizes or burns. Many Awakened die young.
- **Heartstones**: crystallized fragments of the Glow, found mostly around the Glow's epicenter in the Pacific. Halcyon
  Dynamics harvests and breaks them. Ye Xuan's stone is **Seed Zero**, the first and only whole heartstone, found at
  the epicenter. It is linked to the bell — a voice of the **Great Dao** that chose Ye Xuan because he came from a
  world with no qi at all, so the Seal has no hold on him.
- Seed Zero has layers. Each time Ye Xuan comprehends a layer, he gains an insight; looking deeper than he is ready for
  overwhelms him (as in chapter 2).
- Some ancient cultivation lineages survived the qi-less ages as useless "breathing exercises" or in sealed sleep:
  Granny Liu's Fujian family manual (Min Mountain School), and the hidden **Kunlun** sect in western China, whose
  elders wake after the Glow.

## Ye Xuan's power: Great Dao Comprehension (大道悟性)

When he focuses on something, he sees the Dao patterns that make it work and understands them completely: a phone, a
traffic light, an engine, a tai chi form, another person's gift. Complex or living things cost more; Seed Zero is an
ocean. He can copy the **technique** behind a gift immediately, and with his own qi he can reproduce a weaker version
of the principle. He can also **unwind** or **stabilize** gift patterns (healing burnout) — the most valuable and most
dangerous thing he can do.

## Cultivation (Ye Xuan reuses the realm names from the novels he read — "at least someone will understand")

1. **Qi Gathering** (聚气), layers 1–9. Draw qi into the dantian below the navel. Ch 5: layer 1 (body expels black
   grime). Ch 14: layer 2. Ch 26: layer 3. Ch 37: layer 4. Ch 56: layer 5. Ch 60: layer 6. Ch 79: layer 7.
   Ch 96: layer 8. Ch 117: layer 9 peak.
2. **Foundation Establishment** (筑基) — ch 145, mid-battle at the Ascension Gala. The foundation is a "Dao Foundation"
   woven from every pattern he has comprehended. Gives inner vision, qi projection a few meters, far cheaper
   comprehension.
3. **Golden Core** (金丹) — Volume 4 (around ch 300). Condenses his comprehension into a single golden seed.
4. **Nascent Soul** (元婴) — Volume 7 (around ch 540).
5. **Spirit Transformation** (化神) — Volume 9.
6. **Void Refinement** and beyond — Volumes 10–12; the last steps are his own, "against the heavens".

Qi is thin indoors, thicker around old trees, old stones, moving water and places the Glow touched hardest. Dawn is
best. His breathing method begins as a mix of the Columbus Park grannies' tai chi breathing and Juno's four-count step
rhythm; with Seed Zero's first layer it doubles. In ch 80 he writes it down as **The Breath of Comprehension**, later
the core of the Mott Street Sect.

Techniques he owns (add new ones here):
- **Four-Count Step** (copied from Juno, ch 3): one, two, three — breathe — four, release. Evolves into his own
  **Threefold Step** (ch 37, no breath delay).
- **Pattern circuits**: Dao patterns etched into copper or wood (ch 9 crude tin box; ch 31 first real etched copper
  plate that hides Seed Zero's pulse). Later qi batteries from Glow-touched ginkgo wood (ch 85).
- **Pattern needles**: etched sewing needles that guide qi into gift patterns to heal (ch 55–56).
- **Unwinding strike**: a qi strike to a gift pattern's node that disables the gift for minutes (ch 52).

## Characters

  federal agents; rule three: understand the ability before it kills you; rule four: food). Loves his mother's braised
