# Comprehension Against the Heavens — outline

12 volumes × 81 chapters = 972 chapters. Chapter beats are detailed through chapter 175; later volumes are planned at
arc level. Whoever writes the next chapters extends the detailed beats one arc at a time (about 20 chapters ahead),
following the volume plan, and keeps `bible.md` up to date.

## Volume plan

1. **Above the Bakery** (1–81). Arrival, first qi, Skyline Couriers, the Canal Street Dogs, Seed Zero's mystery,
   the Respira trial, Mr. Ash. Ends with Qi Gathering 7 and the joke-founding of the Mott Street Sect.
2. **Pattern and Foundation** (82–162). Hudson Polytechnic, pattern circuits, the Pit tournament, the Ascension
   Gala, Foundation Establishment (ch 145), the bell's warning (ch 149), the Registration Act passes (ch 162).
3. **Registration** (163–243). The 90-day countdown, suppression collars, the underground school, Bureau raids, the
   Min Mountain School survivors in Flushing, Priya's new identity, Halcyon rebuilding abroad. Ye Xuan chooses his Dao
   for Golden Core. Ends with the first collar raid on Chinatown and the sect escaping with its students.
4. **The Ascension Program** (244–324). Halvorsen returns with Celestial backing; artificial Awakened army; Ward's
   and Lily's arc; Kroll turns against Vance; Golden Core breakthrough (~300); Halcyon Tower falls; Vance exposed; the
   Registration Act amended. A Celestial envoy is glimpsed for the first time.
5. **Across the Pacific** (325–405). Ye Xuan flies to Hangzhou; meets this world's parents (emotional core); China's
   Ministry of Special Affairs and Commissioner Bai Chen; the Glow epicenter expedition; proof that Earth had
   cultivators and was sealed. Juno comes along.
6. **Kunlun** (406–486). The waking Kunlun sect; Lu Tianyi rivalry; ancient rules vs. a modern sect; the history of
   the Seal; Ye Xuan earns Kunlun's archive; prepares for Nascent Soul.
7. **The Second Glow** (487–567). A second, partial Glow; heartstone rain; millions Awaken at once and burn out;
   the Mott Street Sect's breathing method spreads worldwide; Nascent Soul (~540).
8. **The Sect of Comprehension** (568–648). The sect becomes an international institution; governments, Halcyon's
   successor, Kunlun conservatives; betrayals; Mateo and Sofia come of age.
9. **Gate in the Sky** (649–729). The Celestial Court's envoys descend; first war; Spirit Transformation; heavy
   losses; Ash's sacrifice.
10. **Dao of Earth** (730–810). Ye Xuan comprehends Earth's own Dao; unites the factions; the truth of the bell and why
   he was chosen; Kroll's last stand.
11. **Against the Heavens** (811–891). War beyond the sky; the Seal is assaulted from both sides; Juno's greatest
   step; the cost of comprehension.
12. **The Road Begins** (892–972). The final battle, the Seal shattered, cultivation opened to all humanity.
   Epilogue (972): dawn tai chi in Columbus Park and egg tarts at the Golden Lotus.
